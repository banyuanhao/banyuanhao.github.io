We released **ReCAST**, a method for assigning reward credit across diffusion timesteps. ReCAST gives each reward more weight at the denoising steps where its feedback is most informative. Read the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13425) and visit our [project page](https://yhangchen.github.io/ReCAST/).

We released our work on **post-training frontier text-to-image models** by combining human preference and rubric rewards. Our recipe improves FLUX.2-dev’s Arena Elo score by **69 points**, and our post-trained Ideogram-4 ranks above every open-source model in the September 4, 2026 Arena leaderboard snapshot. Read the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02967), explore our [project page](https://banyuanhao.github.io/Post-Training-Frontier-Text-to-Image-Models-by-Composing-Preference-and-Rubric-Rewards/), and check out our [X post](https://x.com/arena/status/2106037792870649995?s=20)!

We released **Stream4D**, which uses 4D reconstruction rewards and motion priors to improve scene consistency and preserve natural motion in streaming video generation. Read the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19556) and explore the demos on our [project page](https://banyuanhao.github.io/Stream4D/).

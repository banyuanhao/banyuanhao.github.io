[Arena-T2I Hard: Benchmarking and Improving Faithfulness with Dependency-Aware Checklist](https://banyuanhao.github.io/Arena-T2I-Hard-Page/) was accepted to **NeurIPS 2026 as a Spotlight**! Our benchmark evaluates how faithfully text-to-image models follow complex real-world prompts. We also introduce a dependency-aware checklist reward to improve prompt adherence during reinforcement learning. Check out the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31711) and [project page](https://banyuanhao.github.io/Arena-T2I-Hard-Page/).
